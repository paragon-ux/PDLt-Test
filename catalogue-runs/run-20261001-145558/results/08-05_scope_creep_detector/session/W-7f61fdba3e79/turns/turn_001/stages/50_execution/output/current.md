User Story 1:
As a user, I want to search for products by name so that I can find what I’m looking for quickly.
Operative entity: "search for products by name"

User Story 2:
As a user, I want the search results to also show my recent orders and recommend similar products based on my purchase history.
Operative entities: "show my recent orders", "recommend similar products"

User Story 3:
As a user, when I click on a product, I want to see real-time inventory levels from all warehouse locations and be able to reserve items for up to 24 hours.
Operative entities: "real-time inventory levels", "reserve items for up to 24 hours"
