Policy Statement Mapping:
1. "We collect your name, email, and browsing history to improve our services." → GDPR Articles 5 (principles), 6(1)(f) (legitimate interests), 13 (information to the data subject).
2. "We share data with our advertising partners." → GDPR Articles 5 (principles), 6 (lawful basis – legitimate interest or consent), 28 (processor obligations), 44‑49 (international transfers if partners are abroad).
3. "You can email us to request deletion of your data." → GDPR Article 17 (right to erasure), Article 12 (right to be informed about exercising rights).
4. "We store data on servers in the United States." → GDPR Articles 44‑49 (transfer of personal data to a third country), Article 27 (representative in the Union).
5. "Children under 13 should not use our service." → GDPR Article 8 (conditions for consent of children), Article 5 (lawfulness, fairness, and transparency).

Uncovered GDPR Requirements (not addressed in the excerpt):
- Article 13 & 14 (information that must be provided to data subjects at collection).
- Article 20 (right to data portability).
- Article 21 (right to object to processing).
- Article 22 (automated individual decision‑making, including profiling).
- Article 30 (records of processing activities).
- Article 33 & 34 (notification of personal data breach).
- Article 35 (data protection impact assessment when processing is likely to result in high risk).
