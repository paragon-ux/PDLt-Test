It is impossible to meet all of the stated requirements simultaneously. A comparison‑based sorting algorithm cannot achieve O(n) worst‑case time complexity for arbitrary inputs; the lower bound for comparison sorts is Ω(n log n). Therefore no algorithm can satisfy the constraints of being comparison‑based, O(n) worst‑case, stable, and in‑place with O(1) extra space for all possible inputs.

Consequently, the design step cannot be completed, and verification of such an algorithm is likewise unattainable. The only feasible deliverable is this explanation of the impossibility.
