A data pipeline processes clickstream events. Currently it runs as a nightly batch job (processes all events from the past 24 hours in one MapReduce job). The team wants to evaluate switching to stream processing (process each event as it arrives).

Given:
- 50 million events per day, ~600 events/second average, 3000 events/second peak
- Events are 500 bytes average
- Current batch job takes 45 minutes to complete
- Downstream dashboards currently refresh once per day

Analyze: (1) the latency tradeoff (batch: 24h + 45min vs stream: seconds), (2) the cost tradeoff (batch: one large compute job vs stream: always-on infrastructure), (3) the complexity tradeoff (batch: simple retry vs stream: exactly-once, watermarks, late data), (4) a recommendation with justification for which approach to use.
