READ the problem description of a unit stick broken at two uniformly random points.
DEFINE random variables X and Y for the break positions, with X < Y.
COMPUTE segment lengths: L1 = X, L2 = Y - X, L3 = 1 - Y.
IDENTIFY L_min as the minimum of L1, L2, L3 and L_max as the maximum of L1, L2, L3.
FORMULATE the condition L_min / L_max ≥ 1/4.
DETERMINE the region in the (X, Y) unit triangle where the condition holds.
CALCULATE the probability as the ratio of the area of that region to the total area of the unit triangle.
RETURN the probability value.
