| Statement | GDPR articles |
|---|---|
| "We collect your name, email, and browsing history to improve our services." | Art. 5(1)(b) purpose limitation ("improve our services" is vague), 5(1)(c) data minimisation (is browsing history necessary?), Art. 6 lawful basis (not stated), Art. 13(1)(c) purposes and legal basis; tracking browsing history generally needs consent (Art. 6(1)(a), Art. 7; plus ePrivacy cookie rules). |
| "We share data with our advertising partners." | Art. 6/7 consent for advertising, Art. 13(1)(e) recipients must be named, Art. 26/28 joint-controller or processor agreements, Art. 21(2)-(3) right to object to direct marketing, Art. 22 if profiling. |
| "You can email us to request deletion of your data." | Art. 17 right to erasure; Art. 12(3) answer within one month; Art. 12(6) identity verification. |
| "We store data on servers in the United States." | Chapter V, Art. 44-46: needs an adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework, Art. 45) or SCCs (Art. 46); Art. 13(1)(f) must disclose the transfer and safeguards. |
| "Children under 13 should not use our service." | Art. 8: the default age for consent to information-society services is 16 (member states may lower it to 13); below that, parental consent is required and age-verification effort is expected. "Should not use" is not a control. |

**Not covered:** lawful basis for each purpose (Art. 6, 13(1)(c)); retention periods (Art. 5(1)(e), 13(2)(a)); rights of access (15), rectification (16), restriction (18), portability (20), objection (21); right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)); controller identity and DPO contact (Art. 13(1)(a)-(b), 37); right to complain to a supervisory authority (Art. 77, 13(2)(d)); security of processing (Art. 32); breach notification (Art. 33-34).
